# Prom Beton ERP — Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** October 26, 2026

[O‘zbekcha versiya pastda](#maxfiylik-siyosati)

This Privacy Policy explains how the **Prom Beton ERP** mobile application ("the App") collects, uses and protects information. The App is operated by **NEXTBRAIN LLC** ("we", "us", "our").

The App is an internal business tool intended only for authorized employees and executives of our company. It is not intended for the general public, and accounts can only be created by our system administrator.

## 1. Information we collect

We collect only the information needed for the App to work:

| Data | Examples | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Account information | Name, email address | Signing in and identifying the user in the App |
| User ID | Internal account identifier | Linking the user to their account and permissions |
| Business data entered in the App | Orders, customers, payments, expenses, prices, vehicle trips | Core functionality of the ERP system |

We do **not** collect location data, contacts, photos, health data, browsing history or advertising identifiers.

## 2. How we use information

Information is used **only** to provide the App's functionality:

- authenticating users and keeping accounts secure;
- displaying and managing orders, customers, payments and reports;
- maintaining, troubleshooting and improving the service.

We do **not** use your information for advertising or marketing, and we do **not** track you across other companies' apps or websites.

## 3. Sharing of information

We do **not** sell, rent or share personal information with third parties for their own purposes. Information may be disclosed only:

- to service providers that host our servers, under confidentiality obligations;
- when required by law or a valid request from a government authority.

## 4. Data storage and security

Data is stored on servers controlled by our company. All communication between the App and the server is encrypted using HTTPS. Access to data is limited to authorized users according to their role. Passwords are never stored in plain text.

## 5. Data retention

We keep account and business data for as long as the user's account is active and as long as required for accounting and legal purposes. When an account is deactivated, the related personal information is deleted or anonymized unless we are legally required to keep it.

## 6. Your rights

You may request to access, correct or delete your personal information, or to deactivate your account, by contacting us at the email below. Because accounts are created and managed by the company administrator, account deletion requests are handled by the administrator.

## 7. Children's privacy

The App is intended for company employees only and is not directed to children under 18. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

## 8. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top shows when it was last changed.

Ilova faqat kompaniyamizning vakolatli xodimlari va rahbariyati uchun mo‘ljallangan ichki biznes vositasidir. U keng ommaga mo‘ljallanmagan va hisoblarni faqat tizim administratori yaratadi.

## 1. Qanday ma’lumotlar yig‘iladi

Biz faqat Ilova ishlashi uchun zarur bo‘lgan ma’lumotlarni yig‘amiz:

| Ma’lumot | Misollar | Maqsad |
|---|---|---|
| Hisob ma’lumotlari | Ism, email manzil | Tizimga kirish va foydalanuvchini aniqlash |
| Foydalanuvchi ID | Ichki hisob identifikatori | Foydalanuvchini hisobi va huquqlari bilan bog‘lash |
| Ilovaga kiritiladigan ish ma’lumotlari | Buyurtmalar, mijozlar, to‘lovlar, xarajatlar, narxlar, transport reyslari | ERP tizimining asosiy funksiyalari |

Biz joylashuv, kontaktlar, rasmlar, sog‘liq ma’lumotlari, brauzer tarixi yoki reklama identifikatorlarini **yig‘maymiz**.

## 2. Ma’lumotlardan qanday foydalaniladi

Ma’lumotlar **faqat** Ilova funksiyalarini ta’minlash uchun ishlatiladi:

- foydalanuvchini tizimga kiritish va hisob xavfsizligini ta’minlash;
- buyurtmalar, mijozlar, to‘lovlar va hisobotlarni ko‘rsatish hamda boshqarish;
- xizmatni qo‘llab-quvvatlash, nosozliklarni bartaraf etish va yaxshilash.

Ma’lumotlaringiz reklama yoki marketing uchun **ishlatilmaydi** va boshqa kompaniyalarning ilova yoki saytlarida siz **kuzatilmaysiz**.

## 3. Ma’lumotlarni uchinchi shaxslarga berish

Biz shaxsiy ma’lumotlarni uchinchi shaxslarga **sotmaymiz**, ijaraga **bermaymiz** va ularning o‘z maqsadlari uchun **uzatmaymiz**. Ma’lumotlar faqat quyidagi hollarda oshkor qilinishi mumkin:

- serverlarimizni joylashtiruvchi xizmat ko‘rsatuvchilarga, maxfiylik majburiyati asosida;
- qonun talab qilganda yoki vakolatli davlat organining qonuniy so‘roviga binoan.

## 4. Saqlash va xavfsizlik

Ma’lumotlar kompaniyamiz nazoratidagi serverlarda saqlanadi. Ilova va server o‘rtasidagi barcha aloqa HTTPS orqali shifrlanadi. Ma’lumotlarga kirish foydalanuvchi roliga ko‘ra cheklanadi. Parollar hech qachon ochiq holda saqlanmaydi.

Hisob va ish ma’lumotlari foydalanuvchi hisobi faol bo‘lgan davrda hamda buxgalteriya va qonunchilik talablari uchun zarur bo‘lgan muddat davomida saqlanadi. Hisob o‘chirilganda, qonun saqlashni talab qilmasa, unga tegishli shaxsiy ma’lumotlar o‘chiriladi yoki anonimlashtiriladi.

## 6. Sizning huquqlaringiz

Shaxsiy ma’lumotlaringizni ko‘rish, tuzatish yoki o‘chirish, shuningdek hisobingizni o‘chirish uchun quyidagi email orqali murojaat qilishingiz mumkin. Hisoblar kompaniya administratori tomonidan yaratilgani va boshqarilgani sababli, hisobni o‘chirish so‘rovlarini administrator ko‘rib chiqadi.
